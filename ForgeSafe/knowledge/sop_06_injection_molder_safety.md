# SOP-06: Injection Molding Machine Safety and Clamp Pressure Protocol

## Standard Reference
ANSI/PLASTICS B151.1 (Safety Requirements for Injection Molding Machines), OSHA 29 CFR 1910.212 (General Requirements for All Machines), and EN 201.

## Purpose and Scope
Applies to hydraulic, hybrid, and electric plastic injection molding machines. Details safety interlocking, nozzle purge hazards, and clamp hydraulic pressure stability.

## Safety Guidelines and Operating Thresholds
- **Hydraulic Clamping Pressure:** Nominal 130.0 - 175.0 bar.
- **Degradation / Leak Warning:** Clamp pressure drops below 110.0 bar during active mold hold cycle.
- **Barrel Heater Zone Temperature:** Within ±5.0°C of recipe setpoint. Deviation > 20.0°C indicates heater band failure or thermocouple disconnect.

## Incident Escalations and Procedures
- **Pressure Degradation / Clamp Locking Anomaly:**
  - Severity: CRITICAL.
  - Risk: Part flashing, molten polymer splatter at high pressure, clamp toggle mechanism damage.
  - Mandatory Action: Trigger Simulated Mold Cycle Halt. Lockout hydraulic pump drive. Ensure safety gate interlocks remain locked until barrel depressurization is completed.
  - Designated Owner: Injection Molding Specialist & Plant Safety Officer.

- **Purge and Thermal Variance Warning:**
  - Severity: HIGH.
  - Mandatory Action: Clear machine perimeter of all operators before barrel purge. Verify ventilation hood extraction.
