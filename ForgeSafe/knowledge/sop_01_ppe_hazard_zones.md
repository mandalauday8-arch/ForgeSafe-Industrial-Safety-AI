# PPE violation
Personal protective equipment (PPE) non-compliance protocol under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.132 and ISO 45001. All personnel must wear ANSI Z89.1 certified hardhats, high-visibility safety vests, and eye protection in active machining, crane transit, and construction cells. Unhelmeted or unvested personnel must be issued immediate corrective action and provided compliant gear before resuming work in the zone.

# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Compliance and Hazard Zone Protocol
## Standard Reference
OSHA 29 CFR 1910.132, 1910.135 (Head Protection), 1910.136 (Foot Protection), 1910.138 (Hand Protection), and ISO 45001:2018 Occupational Health and Safety Management.

## Purpose and Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure defines mandatory personal protective equipment requirements and perimeter zone restrictions for all personnel operating within or traversing active manufacturing, machining, and construction work cells.

## Required Protective Equipment by Zone
1. **Zone A (General Operational Floor):** High-visibility safety vest (Class 2 minimum), steel-toed boots, protective safety glasses with side shields.
2. **Zone B (Active Machining Cells - Lathes, Mills, Presses):** ANSI Z89.1 Type 1 hardhat, eye protection, ear protection (>85 dBA areas), cut-resistant gloves when handling raw stock, and safety vest.
3. **Zone C (Thermal, Furnace & Chemical Hazard Zones):** Heat-resistant aluminized apron/sleeves, face shield over safety glasses, thermal gloves, organic vapor/particulate respiratory mask (N95/P100), and hardhat.

## Non-Compliance Protocol and Escalations
- **Missing Hardhat (NO-Hardhat):** If an unhelmeted worker is identified within Zone B or C:
  - Immediate auditory hazard alert.
  - Automatic work cell hold or safety pause.
  - Severity: HIGH. If worker is within 2 meters of active machinery: CRITICAL.
  - Mandatory Action: Notify Site Safety Lead and dispatch area steward to issue compliant head protection before work resumes.

- **Missing Safety Vest (NO-Safety Vest):** In low-visibility or heavy machinery forklift transport zones:
  - Severity: HIGH.
  - Mandatory Action: Request worker immediately don standard high-visibility safety vest. Record infraction in safety compliance ledger.

- **Missing Protective Mask (NO-Mask):** In fine-particulate grinding, aerosolized coolant mist, or chemical vapor areas:
  - Severity: MEDIUM to HIGH based on active mist concentration.
  - Mandatory Action: Issue respiratory protection notice and verify exhaust ventilation operation.

## Restricted Area Entry and Proximity
- Unauthorized entry into marked red-line safety envelopes around automated machinery triggers an immediate simulated safety interlock or equipment pause.
- Safety officers must clear and reset the perimeter interlock once the zone is verified clear.
