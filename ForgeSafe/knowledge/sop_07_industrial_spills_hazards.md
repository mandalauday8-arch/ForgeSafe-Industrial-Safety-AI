# SOP-07: Industrial Spill Containment, Hazard Cordoning, and Restricted Entry

## Standard Reference
OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120 (Hazardous Waste Operations and Emergency Response - HAZWOPER), EPA 40 CFR Part 112 (Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure), and NFPA 400.

## Purpose and Scope
Prescribes immediate containment protocols, PPE mandates, and perimeter barricading for oil, chemical, hydraulic fluid, or hazardous liquid spills detected on plant floors or adjacent walkways.

## Spill Classification and Action Levels
- **Minor Machine Leaks (< 5 Liters of ISO Oil / Coolant):**
  - Severity: MEDIUM.
  - Mandatory Action: Deploy absorbent socks around the base of the machine. Apply floor absorbent granules. Place yellow caution safety cones at a 2-meter perimeter.

- **Major Hydraulic / Chemical Spill (> 5 Liters or Active Spray):**
  - Severity: CRITICAL.
  - Risk: Slip and fall hazard, electrical fire, hydraulic fluid atomization, toxic vapor inhalation.
  - Mandatory Action: Initiate immediate simulated work zone evacuation and equipment emergency stop. Barricade entry doors. Dispatch certified HAZMAT spill response team equipped with chemical-resistant nitrile boots and organic vapor respirators.
  - Designated Owner: Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) Lead & Area Supervisor.

## Restricted Area Entry Monitoring
- Unescorted entry into high-voltage switchgear enclosures, robot cells, or overhead crane transport corridors requires safety interlock authorization.
- Vision or sensor triggers in unauthorized zones mandate an instant work pause until verified clear.
