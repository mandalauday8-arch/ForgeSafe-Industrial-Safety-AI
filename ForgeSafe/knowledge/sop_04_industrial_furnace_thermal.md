# SOP-04: Industrial Furnace Thermal Management and Overheating Shutdown

## Standard Reference
NFPA 86 (Standard for Ovens and Furnaces), OSHA 29 CFR 1910.119 (Process Safety Management), and ISO 13577 (Industrial Furnaces and Associated Processing Equipment).

## Purpose and Scope
Covers operational safety, combustion monitoring, heat index management, and thermal runaway emergency procedures for industrial melting, heat-treating, and forging furnaces.

## Thermal Thresholds and Sensor Indicators
- **Normal Operating Core Temperature:** 65.0°C - 95.0°C (external skin / sensor casing).
- **Elevated Warning Thermal State:** > 110.0°C or Heat Index > 0.85.
- **Critical Overheating / Thermal Runaway State:** > 135.0°C or Heat Index > 0.95 with acoustic combustion instability (> 85 dB).

## Emergency Response Procedure
- **Overheating Degradation / Thermal Runaway:**
  - Severity: CRITICAL.
  - Risk: Refractory wall breakdown, runaway combustion, structure fire, toxic outgassing.
  - Mandatory Action: Trigger Emergency Fuel Cutoff & Simulated Furnace Shutdown. Evacuate 15-meter furnace perimeter zone. Activate auxiliary radiant cooling fans and monitor casing temperature until stabilized below 80°C.
  - Designated Owner: Thermal Process Supervisor & Facility Safety Chief.

- **Moderate Heat Index Anomaly:**
  - Severity: HIGH.
  - Mandatory Action: Verify cooling water jacket flow, inspect thermocouple calibration, and throttle heating element power by 25%.
