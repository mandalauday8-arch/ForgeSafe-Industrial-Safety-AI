# SOP-02: Hydraulic Press and High-Pressure Fluid Safety

## Standard Reference
ISO 4413:2010 (Hydraulic Fluid Power Safety Requirements), OSHA 29 CFR 1910.217 (Mechanical Power Presses), and ANSI B11.2.

## Purpose and Scope
Defines operating safety parameters, pressure failure thresholds, and emergency shutoff procedures for industrial hydraulic presses and high-pressure forming machinery.

## Baseline Thresholds and Warning Limits
- **Nominal Hydraulic Pressure:** 140.0 - 180.0 bar.
- **Warning Level (Degrading Pressure):** < 120.0 bar or sudden fluctuations > 25 bar/min.
- **Critical Threshold (Pressure Loss / Seal Failure):** < 95.0 bar or pressure spike > 220.0 bar.
- **Hydraulic Fluid Temperature Limit:** 65.0°C maximum. Above 75.0°C indicates internal valve cavitation or oil breakdown.

## Emergency Protocols and Actions
- **Pressure Degradation / Rapid Pressure Loss:**
  - Severity: CRITICAL.
  - Risk: Ram drop, die binding, catastrophic hose rupture, injection injury.
  - Mandatory Action: Trigger immediate Emergency Stop (E-Stop) / Simulated Shutdown. Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) hydraulic supply line. Depressurize accumulator tank to zero bar before inspection.
  - Designated Owner: Site Supervisor & Senior Maintenance Engineer.

- **Moderate Pressure Anomaly / Fluid Level Drop:**
  - Severity: MEDIUM to HIGH.
  - Mandatory Action: Schedule inspection of proportional valves, hydraulic cylinders, and seal manifolds before next operational shift. Top up ISO VG 46 hydraulic fluid.
